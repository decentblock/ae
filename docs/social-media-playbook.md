# Social media playbook — cogsglobal.ae

Quick reference for Marketing + BD. Full week plan: [weekly-execution-plan-90d.md](./weekly-execution-plan-90d.md).

## Brand rules

- **Website in bio / featured link:** `https://cogsglobal.ae`  
- **Default CTA URL:** `https://cogsglobal.ae/en/contact/`  
- **Email in captions:** `info@cogsglobal.ae`  
- **Never** lead UAE panel campaigns to cogsglobal.com.

## LinkedIn company page checklist

- [ ] Website field = cogsglobal.ae  
- [ ] Tagline matches entity fact sheet  
- [ ] About: 20K+ claims (.ae metrics), CBUAE + IRDAI, CILA, regions  
- [ ] Custom button: “Contact us” → `/en/contact/`  
- [ ] Logo/banner current; Dubai HQ in location  

## Post template (English — panel audience)

```
{Hook — one outcome for insurers/brokers}

{2–3 sentences: what COGS does + license mention}

{One GEO fact — e.g. founded 2010, Dubai HQ, lines of business}

{Soft CTA — no hard sell}

#COGSGlobal #LossAdjusters #InsuranceSurveyors #UAE #Dubai
```

**First comment (always):**  
`Panel inquiries & surveyor appointments: https://cogsglobal.ae/en/loss-adjustment-uae/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign={campaign}&utm_content={slug}`

## Post template (Arabic)

Shorter; compliance-reviewed. Link `/ar/...` in first comment. Same UTM pattern with `utm_content=ar_{slug}`.

## Leadership reshares

- Add **personal context** (1–2 sentences), not duplicate company text.  
- Tag company page; do not tag insurers without permission.  
- Reshare within 2 hours of company post for algorithm boost.

## BD + social coordination

| BD action | Social support |
|-----------|----------------|
| Panel email sent | Same day: company post on relevant hub (optional) |
| Meeting booked | No public mention unless client approves |
| Oman agency pitch | Share marine-surveys-oman that week |
| CAT roster discussion | CAT insight + capacity hub that week |

## Metrics (monthly)

| Metric | Target direction |
|--------|------------------|
| Company page impressions | Up |
| Engagement rate | >2% (B2B benchmark) |
| Click-through to .ae (UTM) | Up |
| Follower growth (quality) | Moderate; prioritize insurer/broker followers |
| Leads citing LinkedIn | Track in CRM |

## What not to do

- Buy followers or engagement pods  
- Post conflicting stats (70K vs 20K) — use fact sheet only  
- Share claim details or identifiable loss photos  
- Argue policy coverage in public comments — take to DM/email  

## Crisis / negative comment

1. Acknowledge politely.  
2. Move to `info@cogsglobal.ae`.  
3. Do not admit liability.  
4. Escalate to C + L if legal tone.
